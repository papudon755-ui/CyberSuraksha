About the platform

What is CyberSuraksha?

A simple, plain-language guide to staying safe online — built for everyday internet users.

Why this exists

Online fraud in India often targets ordinary people — not because they are careless, but because the tricks are designed to confuse anyone in a hurry. CyberSuraksha explains those tricks in simple English, with pictures, practice games and a short quiz, so that spotting a scam becomes second nature.

Who it is for

Made for real people

🎓 Students

Young users who live on their phones — shopping, UPI payments and social media — and need to recognise fake links, QR scams and job frauds.

👪 Parents & elders

Family members who may be new to digital banking and are often targeted by OTP calls and fake customer-care numbers.

📱 First-time internet users

Anyone just coming online, who deserves safety guidance in clear, jargon-free language from day one.
