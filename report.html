Act fast

Report a cyber fraud

If you lost money or shared sensitive details, do these three things right now. The earlier you report, the better the chance of stopping the fraud.

Step 1 — call now
1930

National cyber helpline — toll-free, 24x7.
Call immediately and describe what happened. Quick reporting helps authorities try to freeze the fraudulent transaction.

2

File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal lets you report online fraud officially:

  1. Visit cybercrime.gov.in
  2. Click "Report Crime" and choose the right category
  3. Fill in the details of what happened, with dates and amounts
  4. Submit and note your acknowledgement number — you will need it to track the complaint
3

Inform your bank immediately

Call your bank's official customer-care number (from their website or app — never from a search-result ad) and ask them to block the transaction or card involved. Share your complaint acknowledgement number with them.

Be prepared

Keep these ready

Having these details at hand makes your complaint faster and stronger.

  • 📷 Screenshots of the fraud messages, links or QR codes
  • 🧾 Transaction ID / UTR number from your bank or UPI app
  • 🕐 Date and time the fraud happened
  • 📞 The fraudster's phone number, UPI ID or account details
Remember: report as early as possible. Do not feel ashamed — scammers are professionals at tricking people, and reporting helps protect others too.
