5 Golden Rules of online safety

If you remember nothing else, remember these five.

  1. Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

    No real bank or company ever asks for them.

  2. Verify before you pay.

    Confirm QR codes and payment requests with the person directly.

  3. Use strong, unique passwords + two-factor authentication.

    Different password for every important account.

  4. Think before you click.

    Check the sender and the real link address first.

  5. Keep your apps and phone updated.

    Updates fix security problems hackers exploit.
