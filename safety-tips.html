Protect yourself

Safety tips that actually work

Simple habits, explained plainly. Follow these and you will avoid the vast majority of common frauds.

The essentials

5 Golden Rules

Print these in your mind. They cover almost every scam described in the threat library.

  1. Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.

    No real bank, delivery company or government office ever asks for them — not on calls, not on SMS, not on WhatsApp. Anyone who asks is a scammer.

  2. Verify before you pay.

    Got a QR code or payment request? Confirm with the person through a separate phone call first. Remember: you never need a PIN to receive money.

  3. Use strong, unique passwords and turn on two-factor authentication.

    Long passwords that you do not reuse, plus a second verification step (like an authenticator app), keep accounts safe even if one password leaks.

  4. Think before you click.

    Check who really sent the message. Long-press a link (or hover on a computer) to see the true address before opening it.

  5. Keep your apps and phone updated.

    Updates fix security holes. Turn on automatic updates so you are protected without having to remember.

Type a password to check its strength.
  • Length matters most. Aim for 12 or more characters.
  • Mix it up. Combine capital and small letters, numbers and symbols.
  • One account, one password. Never reuse an important password on another site.

Easy trick: use a passphrase

Four random words are easier to remember and harder to crack than one tricky word — for example, correct horse battery staple style phrases you invent yourself. Add a number and a symbol to make it stronger.
